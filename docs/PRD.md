# LafiyaTrack Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Information

**Product Name:** LafiyaTrack

**Company:** A&M TECH FOUNDRY

**Version:** MVP v0.1

**Status:** Planning

---

# Vision

To improve child nutrition outcomes by empowering frontline health workers with an offline-first digital platform for screening, referral, follow-up, and nutrition data management.

---

# Mission

To help Community Health Extension Workers (CHEWs) identify and manage childhood malnutrition efficiently, even in communities with poor or no internet connectivity.

---

# Primary Users

- Community Health Extension Workers (CHEWs)
- Supervisors
- Health Facility Administrators

---

# Target Beneficiaries

- Children under five
- Parents and caregivers
- Primary Health Centres
- Local Government Health Authorities
- NGOs and Development Partners

---

# MVP Features

- User Login
- Child Registration
- Nutrition Screening
- MUAC Assessment
- Bilateral Edema Assessment
- Referral Management
- Follow-up Tracking
- Offline Data Collection
- Data Synchronization
- Dashboard and Reports

---

Project planning completed.
